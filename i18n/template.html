FAQ

Questions tilers ask

How accurate is the tile count?

The app works out the full tiles and the edge cuts for each area, then adds the waste factor for the pattern you chose. With PRO, coming soon, it will lay the real pattern over your room and count every cut piece.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Everything is calculated on your phone, so it works in a basement, a new build or anywhere without signal.

Can it handle L-shaped or irregular rooms?

Room shapes are part of PRO, coming soon. You will be able to pick L, T, U or Z shapes, set each wall, and drag corners to add notches and bumps for chimney breasts, pillars or door recesses. Today you can add several rectangular areas to one job.

How is grout and adhesive worked out?

The grout and adhesive calculator is coming with PRO. Grout uses the manufacturers' formula from tile size, tile thickness and joint width; adhesive uses typical coverage for the trowel notch, picked automatically from the tile size. Both include 10% extra and are rounded up to whole bags. You can see it in the PRO preview above.

Can I send a quote to my customer?

PDF quotes are coming with PRO: an estimate with the room drawing, tile counts, materials and your prices, ready to share by email, WhatsApp or any app.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes everything. Backup to a file you control is coming with PRO.
